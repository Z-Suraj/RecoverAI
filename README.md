# RecoverAI

### AI-Powered Revenue Recovery Platform

RecoverAI is an AI-powered revenue recovery platform designed to help businesses identify revenue at risk, understand payment failures, find recovery opportunities, and execute recovery workflows.

> **Don't just identify lost revenue. Take action to recover it.**

---

## 🚀 Overview

Failed payments can create significant revenue leakage for businesses.

However, a failed payment does not always mean that the revenue is permanently lost. Some transactions may still have a recovery opportunity.

RecoverAI turns this problem into an actionable workflow:

**Payment Failure → AI Analysis → Recovery Opportunity → AI Recommendation → Execute Recovery → Revenue Recovery**

Instead of only reporting failed payments, RecoverAI helps businesses understand what happened and decide what to do next.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Businesses can lose revenue because of:

- Failed payments
- Checkout failures
- Payment-related issues
- Customers abandoning transactions
- Recoverable payment opportunities being missed

When these events happen at scale, manually reviewing every transaction becomes difficult.

RecoverAI addresses this problem by bringing payment intelligence and recovery actions into a single platform.

---

## 💡 Solution

RecoverAI provides a centralized recovery platform where businesses can:

1. Monitor revenue at risk
2. Identify recoverable transactions
3. Analyze payment failure patterns
4. Interact with an AI Recovery Copilot
5. Prioritize recovery opportunities
6. Execute recovery workflows
7. Analyze recovery performance
8. Simulate recovery scenarios
9. Track important activities through an audit log

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 📊 Revenue Recovery Dashboard

The dashboard provides a quick overview of the recovery situation.

It displays metrics such as:

- Revenue at Risk
- Recovered Revenue
- Recoverable Pipeline
- Recovery Success Rate
- Active leakage cases
- Recovery opportunities

This allows businesses to understand their revenue situation quickly.

---

### 🤖 AI Recovery Copilot

The Recovery Copilot allows users to interact directly with the AI.

Users can ask questions related to:

- Payment failures
- Transactions
- Customers
- Revenue leakage
- Recovery opportunities
- Recovery decisions

Instead of manually searching through large amounts of data, users can communicate with the AI to understand the situation and receive useful recommendations.

---

### ⚡ Execute Revenue

Execute Revenue is one of the core features of RecoverAI.

After identifying a recovery opportunity, the user can move from **AI recommendation to recovery action** through the platform.

The goal is to avoid simply reporting a problem and instead provide an actionable recovery workflow.

**Identify → Analyze → Recommend → Execute**

---

### 📋 Recovery Queue

The Recovery Queue organizes payment recovery opportunities that require attention.

It helps users focus on important recovery cases instead of manually checking every failed transaction.

Different situations can require different recovery actions, such as:

- Payment retry
- Payment method update
- Customer communication
- Other recovery workflows

---

### 💳 Transaction Management

The Transactions section allows users to inspect individual payment events.

It provides transaction-level context that can help the business understand:

- What happened
- Why attention may be required
- Whether a recovery opportunity exists
- What action may be appropriate

---

### 📈 Analytics

The Analytics section helps businesses understand recovery performance and revenue leakage.

It can be used to identify:

- Revenue loss patterns
- Payment failure patterns
- Recovery performance
- Recovery opportunities
- Overall recovery trends

---

### 🧪 Simulation

The Simulation environment allows recovery scenarios to be tested in a controlled environment.

This helps evaluate recovery workflows before applying them to real-world situations.

---

### 🔍 Audit Log

The Audit Log provides visibility into important activities within the recovery workflow.

This makes it easier to review actions and understand what happened during the recovery process.
